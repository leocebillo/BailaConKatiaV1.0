What to expect

Each class follows the same structure so you always know where you are.

  1. Warm-up. Ten minutes to loosen up and find the beat.
  2. Technique. New steps broken down slowly, with partner rotation.
  3. Social dance. Put it to music with no pressure.

Questions

Do I need a partner?

No. We rotate partners during class.

What should I wear?

Comfortable clothes and shoes you can pivot in. Avoid sneakers with heavy grip.

Are classes in English or Spanish?

Both. Katia teaches in English y español.

Can I try one class first?

Yes. The drop-in pass is made for that.
